About the system

One program for the counter, the floor, the kitchen and the store room.

OKV RMS is a management and point-of-sale system from OKV Technology Consults, a Nigerian software company. It replaces paper bills, order books, stock notebooks and scattered spreadsheets with one program that everyone on your team can use from a normal web browser.

Run a restaurant, a supermarket, or both under one roof. Switch the mode in Settings and the screens adapt: tables and kitchen tickets for the restaurant side, barcode checkout and shelf stock for the shop side.

How it works

From download to first sale in about five minutes

One download. One setup file. No technician needed.

1
Fill the short form

Tell us who you are so we can help if you get stuck. The download starts straight away.

2
Unzip and run the setup

Windows: open the download and double-click OKV_RMS_Setup.exe, then press Next until it finishes. Mac: open the .dmg and drag OKV RMS into Applications. Nothing else needs to be installed first.

3
Sign in and choose your business type

The program opens in your browser. Open Settings and pick Restaurant, Supermarket or Both.

4
Add products and start selling

Add items by hand or import a CSV file. Other tills and waiters connect with the address shown on the server.

5
Activate when you are ready

Before the trial ends, pay once, upload your receipt inside the program, and receive your activation code by email within 24 hours. See the steps.

What is inside the ZIP file
  • OKV_RMS_Setup.exe: the installation wizard (Windows)
  • OKV RMS .dmg: the Mac installer (separate download)
  • Installation and Quick Start Guide (PDF)
  • 5 user manuals (PDF): Admin, Manager, Cashier, Waiter, Kitchen
  • sample_products.csv: an example to import
  • READ_ME_FIRST.txt

Works on Windows 7, 8, 10 and 11 (32-bit and 64-bit) and on Mac. 2 GB of memory is enough. No Python or other software is needed.

Features

Everything the counter, the floor and the store room need

Switch on Restaurant, Supermarket or Both in Settings.

01
Barcode checkout

Scan or type. Enter 3*barcode for a quantity. Sell by kg, litre or piece, hold a sale, and reprint any receipt.

02
Tables and dine-in orders

See which tables are free or busy. Waiters add items with notes like "no pepper" and add more later.

03
Kitchen screen

Orders show up in the kitchen the moment they are sent. Start, Ready, Served. Late tickets turn red after 20 minutes.

04
Stock control

Stock drops with every sale. Receive deliveries, count shelves, see low-stock items and the history of every movement.

05
Cash, POS and transfer

Split one bill across cash, card/POS and bank transfer. Change is worked out for you. VAT and service charge are automatic.

06
Profit reports

Sales, gross profit, expenses and net profit. Best sellers, sales by category, by cashier and by payment method.

07
Staff roles

Waiters cannot take payment. Kitchen sees only the kitchen. Only managers can void a sale. Each role has its own PDF manual.

08
Suppliers, expenses, CSV import

Keep supplier details, record daily expenses, and load hundreds of products from one spreadsheet file.

09
Safe and offline

Runs on your own PC and shop network. A backup is saved every day, and you can download one at any time.

10
Dashboards and charts

Every screen opens with KPI cards and charts: sales by day and hour, best sellers, payment mix, stock value, expenses.

11
Team messages

Staff message each other or everyone from inside the system. Unread counts show in the menu.

12
Cash-up and shifts

Open a shift with a float, close it by counting the drawer, and see exactly how much is over or short. Print the report.

13
Staff control

The admin creates, suspends, deletes and resets staff passwords. Forgotten password? Built-in recovery steps.

14
Export to Excel

One click turns any list (orders, products, expenses, reports) into a spreadsheet file.

15
Quick search and dark mode

Press Ctrl+K to jump to any page, product or order. Switch to dark mode for night shifts.
